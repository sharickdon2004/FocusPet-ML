Project Context

Problem Statement & Justification

PROBLEMATIC

In Colombia, procrastination represents a problem that mainly affects students and young people, as it causes poor academic performance, loss of productivity, and difficulties in fulfilling responsibilities. The excessive use of social media and digital platforms has increased distractions, generating stress, poor time management, and unhealthy habits that impact both educational performance and personal well-being, highlighting the low performance and limited progress of educational institutions.

Why We Chose This Problem

An application makes it possible to provide reminders, task tracking, and motivational tools that can help reduce procrastination and increase academic productivity. In this way, it seeks to offer an accessible and innovative solution that helps users develop discipline, improve their performance, and strengthen their personal and educational well-being. Likewise, it allows easy access for students and young people, since in the 21st century almost everyone has access to a mobile device.
