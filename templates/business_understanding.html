Challenge

1. Problem Statement

In the digital age, screen addiction and lack of productivity are growing concerns, especially among youth and students. Continuous digital distractions lead to reduced attention spans, poor academic performance, and degraded mental health. There is a need for a gamified solution that encourages healthy digital habits.

Screen addiction • Mental health • Gamified solution
Goals

2. Project Objectives

  • Primary: Predict user adaptation and engagement (Beta Rating) using machine learning algorithms based on their digital behavior metrics.
  • Secondary: Identify which behaviors (e.g., successful sessions, trivia participation) most strongly correlate with healthy digital habit formation.
  • Product Goal: Use these insights to personalize the FocusPet app, keeping users motivated through their virtual pet (Rangel).
Viability

3. Project Feasibility

Technical Feasibility

High. Structured data with Supervised ML algorithms (Regression & Classification).

Data Feasibility

Beta-testing dataset with real user interactions, screen times, and ratings.

Business Value

Proactively assist at-risk users (Rating 1-2) before they abandon the app.
