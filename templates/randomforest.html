How does Random Forest work?

Unlike a single decision tree (which can be imprecise or biased), a Random Forest trains dozens of independent trees. Each tree analyzes a random combination of variables and "votes" for a rating.

The final prediction corresponds to the majority decision of the forest. This drastically reduces error and prevents overfitting.
