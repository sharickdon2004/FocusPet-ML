🌲 What is Random Forest?

Random Forest is an ensemble learning method that creates multiple decision trees (typically 100+) and combines their predictions. Each tree "votes" for a rating, and the most voted rating becomes the final prediction. This approach significantly reduces overfitting and improves accuracy compared to a single decision tree.

📈 Why Random Forest?

🌳 Single Decision Tree

  • ❌ Prone to overfitting
  • ❌ Sensitive to data variations
  • ❌ Less stable predictions
  • ❌ No confidence score

🌲 Random Forest

  • ✅ Reduces overfitting significantly
  • ✅ More robust and stable
  • ✅ Higher accuracy (5-10% better)
  • ✅ Provides confidence probabilities
