Model Engineering

Training, Hyperparameter Tuning, and Validation of 3 ML Models

Linear Regression Decision Tree Random Forest

We trained three distinct supervised learning models to determine which algorithmic approach best captures the patterns of digital addiction recovery and gamification engagement.

1. Linear Regression
Regression

Statistical baseline. Assumes linear relationship between user behavior and final rating. Output is continuous, rounded to nearest integer rating (1-5).

Hyperparameters:
  • fit_intercept=True
  • Standard Ordinary Least Squares (OLS)
2. Decision Tree
Classification

Non-linear model that splits dataset based on feature thresholds. Highly interpretable but prone to overfitting.

Hyperparameters:
  • criterion="gini" (Measuring impurity)
  • max_depth=5 (Restricted to prevent overfitting)
  • random_state=42
3. Random Forest
Ensemble Classification

Most robust model. Builds multiple decision trees on random subsets and averages their votes. Lowers variance and improves prediction stability.

Hyperparameters:
  • n_estimators=100 (Assembly of 100 trees)
  • max_depth=10 (Deeper due to ensemble nature)
  • random_state=42
