Phase 4: Model Engineering

Training, Hyperparameter Tuning, and Validation of 3 ML Models.

We trained three distinct supervised learning models to determine which algorithmic approach best captures the patterns of digital addiction recovery and gamification engagement.

1. Linear Regression

Type: Regression

Used as our statistical baseline. It assumes a linear relationship between user behavior and the final rating. The output is a continuous number that we rounded to the nearest integer rating (1-5).

Hyperparameters:
  • fit_intercept=True
  • Standard Ordinary Least Squares (OLS)
2. Decision Tree

Type: Classification

A non-linear model that splits the dataset based on feature thresholds (e.g., "Are successful sessions > 15?"). Highly interpretable but prone to overfitting.

Hyperparameter Tuning:
  • criterion="gini" (Measuring impurity)
  • max_depth=5 (Restricted to prevent overfitting and memorize noise)
  • random_state=42
3. Random Forest

Type: Ensemble Classification

Our most robust model. It builds multiple decision trees on random subsets of data and averages their votes. This significantly lowers variance and improves prediction stability.

Hyperparameter Tuning:
  • n_estimators=100 (Assembly of 100 trees)
  • max_depth=10 (Deeper allowed due to ensemble nature)
  • random_state=42
