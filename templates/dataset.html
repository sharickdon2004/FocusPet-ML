FocusPet Beta Dataset

Comprehensive technical overview and complete testing records metrics retrieved from our 55 evaluation beta-testers framework.

Dataset Dimensions & Structure

The dataset is strategically organized across four foundational operational axes. These metrics allow the optimization of statistical patterns and predictive Machine Learning models targeting productivity behaviors:

  • Identification & Demographics: Basic individual indicators tracking tracking variance patterns.
  • Performance & Efficiency Metrics: Direct comparative measurement of active screen allocations and application boundaries workflows.
  • Engagement & Gamification: Quantifiable behavioral interaction feedback loops paired with the in-app reward infrastructure.
  • Target Outcome Variable: Final descriptive evaluation feedback score provided directly by the user.

Variables Dictionary

  • 🔹 usuario_id: Unique numerical registration identity code assigned per tester.
  • 🔹 edad: Participant integer age ranging from a spectrum of 13 to 30 years old.
  • 🔹 horas_pantalla_pre: Baseline daily average screen usage time hours prior to deployment.
  • 🔹 horas_pantalla_post: Observed daily average device screening usage during the active trial.
  • 🔹 sesiones_exitosas: Completed focus timers segments carried through without anomalies.
  • 🔹 sesiones_fallidas: Abandoned intervals or attempts accessing blocked apps.
  • 🔹 interacciones_rangel: Volumetric action clicks and attention inputs dedicated to the pet companion.
  • 🔹 trivias_ganadas: Correct educational quiz challenge milestones solved.
  • 🔹 recompensas_canjeadas: Total custom perks unlocked using virtual coin assets.
  • ⭐ calificacion_beta [TARGET]: User validation score ranging from a metric scale of 1 to 5.
